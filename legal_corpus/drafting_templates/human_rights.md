## Addressee
To
The Registrar (Law),
[National Human Rights Commission, Manav Adhikar Bhavan, C-Block, GPO Complex, INA, New Delhi - 110023 / State Human Rights Commission, [STATE]].

## Subject
Subject: Complaint of violation of human rights by [PUBLIC SERVANT / AUTHORITY] — [CUSTODIAL VIOLENCE / ILLEGAL DETENTION / POLICE ATROCITY / NEGLIGENCE].

## Victim and complainant
Name, age, gender, address of the victim; relationship of the complainant to the victim.

## Authority complained against
Name, designation and office of the public servant(s), and the State.

## Facts
Date, place, what happened, injuries, medical treatment, witnesses; complaints made to other authorities.

## Declaration on other proceedings
The matter is not pending before any court or other commission, and the incident occurred within the last one year (Section 36 of the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993).

## Relief sought
Enquiry, compensation, action against the officials responsible, and protection.

## Closing
Place / Date / Signature / Name. Enclosures.

## Filing notes
File online on HRCNet (hrcnet.nic.in) or by post. A complaint about an incident in a State may also be filed with that State's Human Rights Commission; the same complaint should not be filed with both.
