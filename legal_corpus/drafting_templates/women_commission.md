## Addressee
To
The Chairperson,
[National Commission for Women, New Delhi / State Commission for Women, [STATE]].

## Subject
Subject: Complaint regarding [DOMESTIC VIOLENCE / HARASSMENT / DOWRY DEMAND / WORKPLACE SEXUAL HARASSMENT / POLICE INACTION] — request for intervention.

## Complainant and victim
Name, age, address and contact of the complainant and, if different, of the aggrieved woman, with her consent.

## Respondents
Name, relationship and address of each person complained against, and the police station / office concerned.

## Facts
Numbered paragraphs with dates, incidents, injuries, threats, demands; complaints already made to police or other authorities with dates and numbers.

## Relief sought
Request the Commission to take cognizance, call for a report from the police / authority, ensure registration of an FIR where offences are disclosed, and provide counselling or legal assistance.

## Closing
Place / Date / Signature / Name. Enclosures.

## Filing notes
The NCW does not take up matters that are sub judice before a court or already before another commission. In an emergency call 112, or the women's helpline 181.
