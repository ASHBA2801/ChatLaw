## Addressee
To
The Hon'ble Chief Minister of [STATE],
Chief Minister's Special Cell / Public Grievance Cell,
Secretariat, [CAPITAL CITY].

## Subject
Subject: Request for intervention regarding [ISSUE] — [DISTRICT] — no redress from [OFFICE(S) ALREADY APPROACHED].

## Introduction
Respected Hon'ble Chief Minister,
I, [PETITIONER NAME], residing at [ADDRESS], mobile [PHONE], respectfully submit this petition.

## Grievance
Numbered paragraphs: the problem, dates, the officials or departments approached with reference numbers, the replies received, and the hardship caused.

## Why intervention is sought
Short explanation of why the matter needs attention at the State level (repeated inaction, urgency, safety, a large number of people affected). No exaggeration.

## Request
I humbly request your kind intervention to direct the [DEPARTMENT / DISTRICT ADMINISTRATION / POLICE] to [SPECIFIC ACTION] and to inform me of the action taken.

## Closing
Place / Date / Signature / Name.
Enclosures: copies of earlier petitions, acknowledgments and supporting documents.

## Filing notes
Most States accept CM cell petitions by post, by email, in person, and through an online grievance portal; the petition is forwarded to the department concerned and a petition number is issued for tracking.
