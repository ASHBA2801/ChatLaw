## Addressee
To
The Station House Officer,
[NAME OF POLICE STATION] Police Station,
[ADDRESS OF POLICE STATION].

Through: in person / registered post / e-FIR portal of the State Police.

## Subject
Subject: Complaint regarding [SHORT DESCRIPTION OF OFFENCE] committed on [DATE] at [PLACE] — request to register a First Information Report under Section 173 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023.

## Salutation and introduction
Respected Sir/Madam,
I, [COMPLAINANT NAME], [son/daughter/wife] of [PARENT/SPOUSE NAME], aged about [AGE] years, residing at [ADDRESS], mobile [PHONE], submit this complaint and state as follows.

## Particulars of the accused
Name(s), description, address and relationship (if any) of each person against whom the complaint is made. Where the identity is not known, state "unknown persons" and give any description, vehicle number, phone number or account number available.

## Statement of facts
Numbered paragraphs in chronological order: date, time and place of each event; what each accused person did; what was said; who witnessed it; loss or injury suffered (with amounts); and how the complainant came to know of it. Facts only — no argument. Each paragraph should be something the complainant can personally vouch for.

## Offences disclosed
The facts disclose cognizable offences, including those punishable under [SECTIONS OF THE BHARATIYA NYAYA SANHITA, 2023 OR SPECIAL LAW]. Only provisions that the retrieved statutory text supports are named; the police are requested to add any other offence the investigation discloses.

## Evidence and witnesses
List of documents, photographs, screenshots, medical certificates, bank statements and names/addresses of witnesses, each marked as an annexure.

## Jurisdiction note
The occurrence took place within the limits of this police station. Where it did not, the complaint asks the officer to register a Zero FIR under Section 173(1) of the BNSS and transfer it to the police station having jurisdiction.

## Prayer
It is therefore requested that you may kindly:
(a) register a First Information Report under Section 173 of the BNSS, 2023 for the offences disclosed;
(b) furnish a copy of the FIR to the complainant free of cost as required by Section 173(2);
(c) investigate the matter and take action against the accused in accordance with law; and
(d) provide protection to the complainant and witnesses where required.

## Closing
Place: [PLACE]
Date: [DATE]
Yours faithfully,
(Signature)
[COMPLAINANT NAME]

Enclosures: as listed above.

## Filing notes
Ask for the FIR number or a receipt (CSR / acknowledgment) when submitting in person. If the officer refuses to register the FIR, the complaint may be sent to the Superintendent of Police / Commissioner of Police under Section 173(4) BNSS, and thereafter an application may be made to the Judicial Magistrate under Section 175(3) BNSS.
