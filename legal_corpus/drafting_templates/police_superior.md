On [DATE] I submitted a written complaint at [POLICE STATION] (acknowledgment/CSR no. [NUMBER], if any) about the offences described below. As of today no FIR has been registered and [no action has been taken / the officer refused to receive the complaint / the officer asked me to settle]. A copy of that complaint is enclosed.

## Statement of facts
Numbered paragraphs with dates, places, the persons involved and what each did; the loss or injury caused; and evidence available.

## Offences disclosed
The facts disclose cognizable offences under [PROVISIONS SUPPORTED BY THE RETRIEVED TEXT].

## Prayer
I request that you may be pleased to:
(a) investigate the case yourself or direct a subordinate officer to investigate it, as provided in Section 173(4) BNSS;
(b) direct registration of an FIR and furnish me a copy;
(c) take appropriate action regarding the refusal to register the FIR; and
(d) ensure protection for me and the witnesses.

## Closing
Place / Date / Signature / Name.
Enclosures: copy of the earlier complaint and acknowledgment; documents relied on.

## Filing notes
Keep proof of sending (registered post receipt or email acknowledgment). It is a precondition for an application to the Magistrate under Section 175(3) BNSS, which must be supported by an affidavit.
