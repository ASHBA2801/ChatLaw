## Addressee
To
The Director / Superintendent of Police,
[Directorate of Vigilance and Anti-Corruption / Anti-Corruption Bureau / Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau], [STATE / DISTRICT DETACHMENT],
[ADDRESS].

## Subject
Subject: Complaint against [NAME AND DESIGNATION OF PUBLIC SERVANT], [OFFICE], for demanding/accepting a bribe / corrupt practice — request for action under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.

## Introduction
Respected Sir/Madam,
I, [COMPLAINANT NAME], residing at [ADDRESS], mobile [PHONE], submit this complaint.

## Public servant concerned
Name, designation, office and the official work pending with or done by the public servant.

## Facts
Numbered paragraphs: the work the complainant needed, when the demand was made, the amount demanded, the words used, any intermediary, any payment already made, and any recording, message or witness. If the demand is still pending, say so clearly — a pending demand allows a trap to be arranged.

## Offences
The facts disclose offences under the provisions of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 supported by the retrieved text.

## Request
I request that you may kindly register the complaint, take action against the public servant in accordance with law, and keep my identity confidential.

## Declaration
I declare that the facts stated are true to my knowledge and that I am willing to cooperate with the investigation.

## Closing
Place / Date / Signature / Name.
Enclosures: documents, screenshots, recordings (on a separate storage device).

## Filing notes
Agencies generally require a signed complaint with the complainant's identity; anonymous or pseudonymous complaints are usually not acted upon. Complaints about Central Government or Central PSU employees go to the CBI or the Central Vigilance Commission instead.
