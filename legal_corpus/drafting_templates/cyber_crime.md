## Addressee
To
The Inspector of Police,
Cyber Crime Police Station, [DISTRICT / CITY],
[ADDRESS].
(Also to be reported on https://cybercrime.gov.in; for money lost, call 1930 immediately.)

## Subject
Subject: Complaint of [ONLINE FINANCIAL FRAUD / UPI FRAUD / IDENTITY THEFT / SEXTORTION / HACKING] on [DATE] — loss of Rs. [AMOUNT].

## Complainant
Name, address, mobile, email.

## Incident details
Date and time; platform, website or app; phone numbers, social media handles, URLs and email IDs used by the fraudster; what was said or done.

## Transaction details
For each transaction: date and time, amount, mode (UPI / card / net banking), transaction / UTR reference, the complainant's bank account, and the beneficiary account / UPI ID.

## Request
Register an FIR, freeze the beneficiary accounts, trace the persons responsible and recover the amount.

## Closing
Place / Date / Signature / Name.
Enclosures: screenshots, bank statements, call logs.

## Filing notes
Report within the "golden hour" on 1930 to improve the chance of freezing funds. Keep the acknowledgment number from cybercrime.gov.in.
