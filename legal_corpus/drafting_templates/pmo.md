## Addressee
To
The Prime Minister's Office (Public Wing),
South Block, Raisina Hill,
New Delhi - 110011.
(To be lodged online at https://pgportal.gov.in — select "Prime Minister's Office" as the Ministry/Department.)

## Subject
Subject: Grievance regarding [ISSUE] — no redress from [STATE / CENTRAL AUTHORITY] despite representations dated [DATES].

## Grievance description
CPGRAMS limits the description field, so the text is short and factual: who the petitioner is; the issue; authorities approached with dates and registration numbers; what remains unresolved; the specific relief sought. Supporting documents are attached as a single PDF.

## Previous representations
List each earlier complaint with authority, date and reference / grievance number.

## Relief sought
One or two sentences stating the exact action requested.

## Closing
Name / Address / Mobile / Email / Date.

## Filing notes
Register on pgportal.gov.in (or the CPGRAMS app), choose the Prime Minister's Office, paste the description, upload the PDF, and note the registration number. If unsatisfied with the final reply, an appeal can be filed on the portal.
