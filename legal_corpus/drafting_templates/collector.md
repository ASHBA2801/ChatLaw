## Addressee
To
The District Collector and District Magistrate,
[DISTRICT] District,
Collectorate, [ADDRESS].

## Subject
Subject: Petition seeking redress regarding [ISSUE — e.g. encroachment of land in Survey No. __ / non-issue of certificate / inaction of a department] — request for enquiry and action.

## Introduction
Respected Sir/Madam,
I, [PETITIONER NAME], residing at [ADDRESS], mobile [PHONE], submit this petition for your kind consideration.

## Background
Who the petitioner is, the property, scheme, service or public matter concerned, and the department or official responsible for it.

## Grievance
Numbered paragraphs: what happened, when, which office was approached earlier (with dates and reference numbers), what reply (if any) was received, and how the petitioner is affected.

## Applicable rules
Only the statutory provisions, government orders or scheme rules supported by retrieved text are named; otherwise the petition asks for action "in accordance with the applicable rules".

## Request
I therefore request that you may be pleased to:
(a) order an enquiry by the [Revenue Divisional Officer / Tahsildar / concerned department];
(b) direct [SPECIFIC ACTION] within a reasonable time; and
(c) inform me of the action taken.

## Closing
Place / Date / Signature / Name.
Enclosures: copies of earlier petitions, replies, documents and photographs.

## Filing notes
Petitions may be handed in at the weekly grievance day meeting, sent by post or email to the Collectorate, or lodged on the State grievance portal. Note the petition number for follow-up.
