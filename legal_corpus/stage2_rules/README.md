# Stage 2 — Rules, notifications, circulars (catalog-first)

Central **rules / notifications / circulars** are catalogued under
`ingestion_stage: stage_2` and `ingestion_status: catalog_only`.

Mass ingest is deferred until a dry-run cost gate passes (see corpus expansion plan).

Parent statutes (e.g. CGST, CPA, EPA) remain Stage 1 / pilot ingest targets.
